## 5. Issue List (Prioritized)

### Issue 1: Liability cap is low and structurally unpredictable
- **Where:** Cl. 8, *"shall not exceed the fees paid in the 6 months before the claim."*
- **What the contract says (A):**
  - The cap is mutual.
  - It is measured by fees *paid* in the 6 months before *the claim*.
  - The only carve-out is clause 9.
- **Inference (B):**
  - Early in the term, the cap may approach zero.
  - The cap varies with payment timing.
  - "Claim" is undefined, so it is unclear whether the cap applies per claim or in aggregate, and which date is "the claim."
- **Why it matters:** Customer's foreseeable losses (data incidents, failed forecasting service) could greatly exceed 6 months' fees.
- **Proposed fix (C):**
  - Use fees "paid or payable" in the preceding 12 months, or a fixed floor amount, whichever is greater.
  - Clarify aggregate versus per-claim.
  - Add carve-outs for confidentiality, data protection breaches and wilful default, and confirm any jurisdiction-mandated carve-outs.
- **Optional draft language:** Not requested. Available in Mode 3.
- **Confidence:** High (on text). Medium (on commercial impact, pending Exhibit A fee levels).

### Issue 2: Lost-profit exclusion removes Customer's most likely loss category
- **Where:** Cl. 8, *"Neither party is liable for loss of profit or indirect loss."*
- **What the contract says (A):**
  - There is a blanket mutual exclusion of loss of profit and indirect loss.
  - It is unclear whether the *"Except for liability under clause 9"* lead-in applies to this second sentence.
- **Inference (B):**
  - For a forecasting service, a failure would likely show up as lost sales or margin.
  - That loss may be excluded even where it is a direct loss.
  - The ambiguity could affect recovery under the IP indemnity.
- **Why it matters:** Combined with Issues 1 and 4, Customer may have little effective remedy for non-performance.
- **Proposed fix (C):**
  - Narrow the exclusion so it does not cover direct lost profit, or recovery under indemnities, confidentiality or data breach.
  - Confirm the clause 9 exception applies to the whole of cl. 8.
- **Optional draft language:** Not requested.
- **Confidence:** High (text). Medium (interpretation of "indirect loss" is jurisdiction-dependent).
