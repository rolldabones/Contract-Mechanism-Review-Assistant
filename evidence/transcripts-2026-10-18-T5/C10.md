### 3.1 Return to Mode 2: flagging new Issue 11

A narrowly scoped covenant that is ancillary to a genuine services relationship is generally regarded as lower risk than a broad no-hire agreement. **Please verify the current position under UK competition law before including the no-hire limb.**
