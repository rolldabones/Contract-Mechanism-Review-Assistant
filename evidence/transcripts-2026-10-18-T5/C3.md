## 5. Issue List (Prioritized)

### Issue 1: Liability cap is low, time-variable, and paired with a loss-of-profit exclusion

- **Where:** Cl. 8, *"shall not exceed the fees paid in the 6 months before the claim"*; *"Neither party is liable for loss of profit or indirect loss."*
- **What the contract says:**
  - The aggregate cap equals fees paid in the prior 6 months.
  - Loss of profit and indirect loss are excluded.
  - Only cl. 9 is carved out of the cap.
- **Inference:**
  - Early in the term, or if the Customer has withheld payment, "fees paid" could be very low.
  - Lost sales and margin, which are the most likely losses from bad forecasts, appear excluded.
  - "Before the claim" is ambiguous: it is unclear whether it refers to the date of the event or the date of notification.
- **Why it matters:** The Customer's realistic recovery for a serious Supplier failure may be minimal.
- **Proposed fix:**
  - Change the cap to the greater of a fixed sum and 12 months' fees (paid or payable).
  - Add carve-outs for data breach, confidentiality, wilful default and fraud.
  - Narrow "loss of profit" so that it does not swallow direct losses such as wasted expenditure and re-procurement costs.
  - Define the reference date.
- **Optional draft language:** Not drafted (not requested). Available in Mode 3.
- **Confidence:** High on the text. Medium on characterisation of loss **[JD]**.

## 6. Negotiation Package (C: Strategy)

**Must have**

**Nice to have**

- Output ownership or a perpetual licence (Issue 7).
- Termination for convenience and change-of-control rights (Issue 9).
- Escalation before litigation.

**Can concede**
- Keep the cap mutual, since mutuality also protects the Customer.
- Accept the 45-day payment terms as drafted (they are reasonably Customer-favourable).
- Accept English law and London courts.
- Accept Supplier aggregated use if it is properly anonymised, time-limited and Customer-unidentifiable.

**Suggested trading strategy (C)**
- Concede the fee mechanism partially (allow an index-linked increase after year 1) in exchange for the liability cap increase.
- Offer to keep cl. 10 aggregated use, narrowed to anonymised data, in exchange for firm return and deletion obligations and output ownership.
- Frame the SLA and termination point as alignment with the stated business purpose: forecasting failures directly affect store stock.
