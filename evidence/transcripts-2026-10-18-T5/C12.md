## 5. Issue List (Prioritized)

### Issue 1: Liability cap may be low and is mis-timed for early-term claims (Priority: Critical)
- **Where:** Cl. 8, *"shall not exceed the fees paid in the 6 months before the claim."*
- **(A) Fact:** The cap is mutual and set at fees *paid* in the 6 months before "the claim." The only exception is liability under Cl. 9.
- **(B) Inference:**
  - Payment terms are 45 days (Cl. 3), so in the first months of the contract fees *paid* may be zero or minimal, and the cap could be nominal.
  - "Total liability" (an aggregate concept) is measured by reference to "the claim" (a singular, per-claim reference). It is ambiguous which claim sets the reference period when there are multiple claims.
  - There are no carve-outs in the provided text for data/confidentiality breach, fraud, wilful misconduct, or death and personal injury. Some of these may not be excludable under English law regardless of drafting. **This is jurisdiction-dependent; lawyer to verify.**
- **Why it matters:** Customer's realistic exposure from a data incident or a failed service likely exceeds 6 months' fees.
- **Proposed fix (plain English):**
  - Set the cap at the greater of a fixed sum or 12 months' fees *paid or payable*.
  - Clarify that the cap is aggregate and how it is calculated.
  - Add carve-outs, uncapped or with a higher separate cap, for breach of data protection, confidentiality and security obligations, and for fraud and wilful misconduct.
  - Add the standard non-excludable liabilities carve-out.
- **Optional draft language:** Not requested. Available in Mode 3.
- **Confidence:** High on the text. Medium on practical impact, because fee levels are unknown (Exhibit A).

### Issue 2: Loss-of-profit exclusion may remove Customer's core loss (Priority: Critical)
- **Where:** Cl. 8, *"Neither party is liable for loss of profit or indirect loss."*
- **(A) Fact:** The exclusion is absolute and mutual. It is unclear whether the opening words "Except for liability under clause 9" also qualify this second sentence.
- **(B) Inference:**
  - For a forecasting service, Customer's primary harm (lost sales, excess stock) may fall within "loss of profit." The exclusion is mutual, but the practical burden is asymmetric, because Supplier's main loss (unpaid fees) is a debt claim and is likely unaffected.
  - If the exclusion applies to Cl. 9, recovery under the IP indemnity could be narrowed.
  - The meaning of "indirect loss" is interpretation-dependent under English law. **Lawyer to verify.**
- **Why it matters:** It may leave Customer without meaningful recourse for the very risk the deal addresses.
- **Proposed fix:**
  - Confirm that the exclusion does not apply to Cl. 9 or to data/confidentiality breaches.
  - Consider specifying categories of recoverable direct loss, such as the costs of re-procurement, data restoration and remediation, and wasted expenditure.
- **Optional draft language:** Not requested.
- **Confidence:** Medium. Characterisation of losses is fact- and jurisdiction-dependent.
