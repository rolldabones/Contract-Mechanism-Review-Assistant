## 5. Issue List (Prioritized)

### Issue 1: Liability cap is low and measured on fees "paid"
- **Where:** (A) Cl. 8: "shall not exceed the fees paid in the 6 months before the claim"
- **What the contract says:** (A) Except for cl. 9 liability, "each party's total liability" is capped at "the fees paid in the 6 months before the claim."
- **Inference:**
  - (B) Fees are payable 45 days after a valid invoice (cl. 3). The amount "paid" early in the term may therefore be small or nil, so the cap could be lowest when implementation risk is highest.
  - (B) The phrases "total liability" and "the claim" leave unclear whether the cap is aggregate or applies per claim.
- **Why it matters:** (B) The Supplier's exposure for service failure or data misuse could be a small fraction of the Customer's losses.
- **Proposed fix:** (C) Set the cap at the greater of a fixed sum and 12 months' fees "paid or payable". State whether the cap is aggregate or per claim. Count from the date the claim arises.
- **Optional draft language:** (C) Not requested.
- **Confidence:** (B) Med. The measure in the text is unambiguous, but its practical size depends on Exhibit A fees and billing, which were not provided.

### Issue 2: The only carve-out from the cap is cl. 9
- **Where:** (A) Cl. 8: "Except for liability under clause 9"
- **What the contract says:** (A) Clause 9 liability is the only stated carve-out. No carve-out for data breach, confidentiality, fraud, wilful default or death/personal injury appears in cl. 8.
- **Inference:**

- **Why it matters:** (B) The Customer supplies "all data" (cl. 1) to the Supplier. That makes data incidents a principal risk.
- **Proposed fix:** (C) Carve out fraud, death/personal injury, wilful default/abandonment and confidentiality breach. Either carve out data-protection/security breaches or place them under a separate higher super-cap. Add a saving for liability that cannot be limited by law.
